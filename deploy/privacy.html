Privacy Policy

This WhatsApp number is a private, single-user assistant operated by its owner for personal use. It is not a commercial service and does not accept messages from the public.

What is collected

Messages sent to this number by its owner are processed to generate a reply. Message content and any attached images may be stored in the owner's private note system. No data from any other person is collected, and messages from numbers other than the owner's are discarded without being read or stored.

Sharing

Message content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate replies, subject to Anthropic's privacy terms. No data is sold or shared with any other third party.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the number's owner by replying on WhatsApp.
